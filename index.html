1- Simple Analgesics: NSAIDs, Paracetamol

How Simple Analgesics Work?

NSAIDs: Work by inhibiting COX-1, COX-2

COX-1 (Cyclooxygenase-1): Involved in protecting the stomach lining and promoting platelet aggregation.

COX-2 (Cyclooxygenase-2): Mainly involved in inflammation and pain mediation.

Paracetamol: Works by inhibiting COX-3

COX-3 (Cyclooxygenase-3): Mainly found in the brain, involved in pain and fever mediation, but its role is still not fully understood.
